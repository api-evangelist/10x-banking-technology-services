At the end of 2022, 10x and London Microservices partnered for an exciting meetup in our London offices. We had around 40 members of the community attending, where 10xers Frederik Brysse and Daniel Oakes shared some insights and lessons from our microservices journey. Jim Fielding, Backend engineer at Attest, closed the night with a practical talk on Testcontainers .
