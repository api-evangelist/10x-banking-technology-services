Migration timelines don't slip because of the technology, they slip when undocumented legacy knowledge surfaces late. 10x Banking's VP Product, Ross Harty , and Principal Product Manager, Arun Chalil , show how near-live migration and client-controlled batching let banks catch that risk early and move with real confidence.
